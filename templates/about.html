Our journey

From one shelf to a shared city.

It started with a tent borrowed from a neighbour. It hasn't stopped since.

  1. 2023

    The idea

    A camping trip needed a tent nobody wanted to buy. The first listing went live that weekend.

  2. 2024

    The neighbourhood grows

    Kathmandu's first lenders signed on. Thousands of items moved from shelves into weekends.

  3. 2025

    Trust, engineered

    Verified profiles, protected payments, and the review system made sharing feel safe.

  4. Today

    10,000+ neighbours

    A growing community across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur — and still just getting started.
